## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Player attacks roll damage on the monster's damage die, so the wielded weapon contributes nothing to damage.

**Current behavior:**
`player_attacks` rolls damage from the **monster's** `damage_die`. The weapon
only affects to-hit (via `enchant`); armor class never affects hit chance and
only does raw damage subtraction on both sides.

**Desired behavior:**
Player attack damage comes from the player's weapon: unarmed is a 1d4 base die;
a wielded weapon contributes its damage die plus its enchant as a flat bonus.
Keep the existing to-hit and AC-subtraction model unchanged by this issue.

**Key interfaces:**
- The player attack roll in the combat module: roll the player's weapon die
  (unarmed = 1d4), add weapon enchant as flat bonus, then existing AC handling
- Weapon item type: its damage die + enchant fields already exist

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] An unarmed player deals 1d4-based damage (existing AC subtraction applied)
- [ ] A +0 weapon with a d6 die deals d6-based damage
- [ ] Weapon enchant adds a flat bonus equal to its value
- [ ] Monster attack damage is unchanged by this fix

**Out of scope:**
- Redesigning hit chance to include AC
- Critical hits, weapon categories
