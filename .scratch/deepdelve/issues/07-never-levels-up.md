## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Killing monsters adds raw XP directly to the field, bypassing the level-up logic, so the player is permanently level 1.

**Current behavior:**
`kill_monster` does a raw `player.xp += xp`. `Player::gain_xp` (which handles
level-up: max_hp/max_ep growth and the full heal) has zero call sites.

**Desired behavior:**
Monster kills route XP through `Player::gain_xp` so level-ups fire with their
max-stat growth and full-heal, and a level-up is logged to the message log.

**Key interfaces:**
- `Game::kill_monster`: replace the raw xp addition with a `player.gain_xp(xp)` call
- `Player::gain_xp`: existing level-up logic, no change expected
- Message log: surface the level-up event as it is already defined

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A kill that crosses a level threshold increases the player's level
- [ ] Level-up applies the existing max_hp/max_ep growth and full heal
- [ ] A level-up message is logged
- [ ] Non-leveling kills still add XP without side effects

**Out of scope:**
- XP curve changes, class-specific leveling perks
